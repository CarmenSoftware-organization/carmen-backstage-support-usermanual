# Profile

## Where to find it and what it is for

Click your name at the top right, then choose **Profile**. See more about this menu in [The Main Screen](/en/getting-started/main-screen).

This page shows your own account. Use it to change your name and phone number, change your password, and see which **business units** (a business unit is one hotel or branch) your account is linked to. This page only changes your own account. To change someone else's account, use [Users](/en/screens/organization/users).

## What you can do here

- **See your profile overview:** the box on the left shows your initials, name, email, **Member since** and **Account ID** (the number the system uses for your account; give it to the support team when you report a problem).
- **Change your profile information:** click **Edit**, then change your name or phone number.
- **Change your password:** click **Change Password**.
- **See your business units:** the **Business Units** box at the bottom lists every business unit linked to your account, with its code and status **Active** or **Inactive**. If there are none, it says **No business units assigned**.

## Fields explained

**Profile Information**

| Field | What it means | Example / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alias Name** | A short name shown instead of your full name where space is tight | Optional |
| **First Name** | Your first name | `Somchai` |
| **Middle Name** | Your middle name, if you have one | Optional |
| **Last Name** | Your last name | `Jaidee` |
| **Telephone** | A number where you can be reached | Optional |
| **Email Address** | Your account's email | Cannot be changed; the field says **Email cannot be changed** |

**Change Password box**

| Field | What it means | Example / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current Password** | The password you use now | Required; it proves it is really you |
| **New Password** | The password you want to use from now on | Required; at least 6 characters |
| **Confirm New Password** | Type the new password again | Required; must match **New Password** exactly |

## Common tasks

**Change your name or phone number**

1. Click **Edit** in the **Profile Information** box.
2. Change the fields you need.
3. Click **Save Changes**. A message confirms that the profile was updated.
4. To discard your changes instead, click **Cancel**. See [Save and Cancel](/en/getting-started/common-actions#save-cancel).

**Change your password**

1. Click **Change Password**. The **Change Password** box opens.
2. Type your **Current Password**.
3. Type a **New Password** of at least 6 characters.
4. Type the new password again in **Confirm New Password**.
5. Click **Update Password**. A message confirms that the password was changed.
6. Use the new password the next time you sign in.

**If you see an error while changing the password**

- **New passwords do not match:** both new-password fields must be exactly the same.
- **Password must be at least 6 characters:** choose a longer password.
- **The change failed:** check that you typed your current password correctly, then try again.

## Cautions

- After you change your password, the old one stops working. Keep the new password somewhere safe.
- If you forgot your current password, you cannot change it on this page. Ask another administrator to set a new password for you on [Users](/en/screens/organization/users).
- This page does not add or remove business units from your account. That is done on [Users](/en/screens/organization/users).
