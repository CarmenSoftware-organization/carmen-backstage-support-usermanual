# Usage Analytics

## Where to find it and what it is for

Left menu, group **Analytics** > **Usage Analytics**

This screen sums up how much people use the Carmen Blue programs. The system counts what users do on screen, such as opening pages and clicking buttons, and shows it as totals, a daily chart and a ranking of the most-opened pages. The screen is view-only. There is nothing to edit.

Words this screen uses:

- **Events** (one action on screen, such as opening a page once or clicking once)
- **Sessions** (one continuous period of use, from when a user starts the program until they stop)
- **Active users** (how many people used the system in the chosen period, each person counted once)

To see individual actions — who did what — go to [Activity Events](/en/screens/analytics/activity-events).

## What you can do here

- **Pick a date range:** **Last 7 days** (the default), **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom**, then choose the **From** and **To** dates. You can pick at most 90 days. All dates are in Thailand time. Below the filters, the screen says which range you are viewing, for example "Viewing Oct 14, 2026 – Oct 20, 2026".
- **Filter** by **Business Unit** (hotel), **Application** (the program people used, such as the website or a mobile app) and **Event type** (**Click** or **Page view**). Every number on the screen changes as soon as you choose.
- **Read the number cards:** **Events**, **Clicks**, **Page views**, **Sessions** and **Active users** for the chosen range.
- **Read the chart** **Sessions & Active users by day**.
- **See the rankings:** **Top pages** (with sessions and users for each) and **Top elements** (the buttons or links clicked most).
- **See details for one page:** click a page name in **Top pages**. The system opens [Activity Events](/en/screens/analytics/activity-events) with all the same filters. This works only if your role can open the Activity Events screen.
- **Export CSV:** you get the daily figures for the chosen range. See [Export to a CSV file](/en/getting-started/common-actions#export).

If the chosen range has no data at all, the screen shows **No events in the selected range**. Try a wider date range or remove filters.

## Fields explained

This screen has no form to fill in, only the filters described above.

## Common tasks

**See how much one hotel used the system last month**

1. In **Date range**, choose **Last 30 days**.
2. In **Business Unit**, choose the hotel.
3. Read the **Active users** and **Sessions** cards.
4. Check the chart for days with unusually low use.

**Find the most-used page and look at the details**

1. Choose the date range you want.
2. Look at the **Top pages** list.
3. Click a page name. The system opens Activity Events filtered to that page.

## Cautions

- This screen is view-only. Nothing here changes data.
- The figures count only the use that the programs send to be recorded. Use from before recording started is not counted.
