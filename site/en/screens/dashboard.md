# Dashboard

## Where to find it and what it is for

**Dashboard** is the first item in the left menu, and it is the page that opens after you sign in.

This page sums up what changed in the system recently, and how many records each area holds. Use it to get an overview before you start work, or to jump straight to a record someone just changed. The page is view-only; there is nothing to edit here.

## What you can do here

- **See recent activity:** the list in the middle shows up to 15 records that were most recently created, updated or published, newest first, grouped under headings such as today and yesterday. Each row shows:
  - the time
  - the record's name and area, for example **Business Units** (a business unit is one hotel or branch) or **Users**
  - what happened: **created**, **updated** or **published** (published is used for news)
  - who changed it, for example **by Super Admin** (when the system knows)
- **Filter the activity by area:** the buttons above the list, such as **All**, **Clusters**, **Business Units**, **Users**, **Applications**, **News** and **Report Templates**. The number on each button is how many activity rows of that area are in the list.
- **Open a record:** click any row and the system opens that record's page.
- **See the overview:** the **Estate** box on the right shows how many records the system manages in total (**records governed**), and a count per area in the form **active / total**, for example `12 / 12`. For **News**, the first number is how many news items are published.
- **Go to an area:** click an area name in the **Estate** box to open that area's list, for example click **Clusters** to open [Clusters](/en/screens/organization/clusters).

## Fields explained

This page has no form to fill in.

## Common tasks

**See who changed what today**

1. Open **Dashboard**.
2. Look at the rows under today's heading. The right-hand column shows who made the change.

**Find a record that was just created in one area**

1. Click an area button above the list, for example **Users**.
2. The list now shows only that area's activity.
3. Click the row you want to open it.
4. Click **All** to see every area again.

**When you see "Couldn't load recent activity." or "Couldn't load estate counts."**

1. Click the retry button inside that box.
2. If it still fails, refresh the browser page, or try again later.

## Cautions

- This page is view-only. Nothing here is irreversible.
- The activity list shows only the latest 15 records. To see a record's full history, open the record and use [Change history](/en/getting-started/common-actions#activity-trail).
- If the system cannot load an area's count yet, the count shows as `-`.
