# Manage Seats

This guide explains seats, how to read how many are used, and how to release or restore a member's seat. Cluster admins can do this.

A **seat** is one person's right to use Carmen Blue. The Cluster has as many seats as its licenses allow. Everyone in every Business Unit takes a seat from the same pool for the whole Cluster.

## Read the seats bar

Open **Users**. At the top you see **CLUSTER SEATS**.

![The Users page and the seats bar](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-seats-01-users-seats.png)

- The big number is the seats in use. The number after it, such as **/ 13 licensed**, is how many the Cluster may use. The picture shows 12 of 13 in use.
- Each block in the bar is one seat. A coloured block is a seat in use.
- The line under the bar, such as **2 seats open**, tells you how many are left.
- When few are left, that line turns orange and reads **Nearing capacity — 1 seat left**.
- When there are none left, it reads **No seats open**.
- The **View licenses** link opens the [Licenses](/en/screens/licences) page.

## Find who holds a seat

Above the member table are three filter buttons, each with a count.

- **All members** is everyone.
- **Holds seat** is people using a seat now.
- **No seat** is people whose seat has been released.

The **Seat** column in the table shows the Business Unit where the person holds a seat.

## Release a seat

Use this when someone no longer needs Carmen Blue and you want the seat back.

1. Find the person in the table and click **...** at the end of their row.
2. Click **Release seat**.
3. Read the question **Release seat?** and click **Release seat**.

   ![Confirm releasing the seat](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-seats-02-release-confirm.png)

::: warning
A person whose seat is released cannot enter the Cluster's Business Units until you restore it. If it is your own account, you lose your own access too.
:::

A message such as **Seat released for ...** appears. Seats in use goes down by one, and the **Seat** column shows **No seat**.

![The seat released](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-seats-03-released.png)

## Restore a seat

1. Find the person (try the **No seat** filter) and click **...** at the end of their row.
2. Click **Restore seat**.
3. Read the message and confirm.

A message such as **Seat restored for ...** appears. The person holds a seat again, and the access to Business Units that was switched off comes back.

![The seat restored](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-seats-04-restored.png)

## When the Cluster is full

When no seat is open, restoring a seat may not work, and you see a message that the seat limit is reached. Do one of these:

- Release the seat of someone who no longer needs it.
- [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) to ask for more seats. You cannot add seats yourself on screen.

If more seats are in use than the Cluster is licensed for, a warning asks you to release seats. Until you do, Carmen Blue cannot save changes in the Cluster's Business Units.

## If something goes wrong

- You see a message that some seat holders are not in the member list. Release their seats from the **Users** tab of the Business Unit. See [A Business Unit](/en/screens/business-unit-detail).
- You are not sure what to do. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
