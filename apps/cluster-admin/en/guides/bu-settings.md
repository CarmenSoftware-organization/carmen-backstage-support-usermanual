# View Business Unit Settings

This guide shows you how to view the settings of a Business Unit (a hotel or branch) and how to edit the hotel and company details that can be edited. Cluster admins can do this. Before you start, know which Business Unit you want.

## Open the Business Unit

1. Open **Business Units** in the left menu.
2. Click the name of the Business Unit.

Under the top of the page you see the tabs **Overview**, **Hotel**, **Company**, **Configuration**, **Licenses** and **Users**.

## View the settings (view only)

1. Open the **Configuration** tab.

   ![The Configuration tab](/screenshots/en/guide-bu-settings-01-configuration.png)

2. Read the values.
   - **Regional formats**, such as **Timezone** and the date and time formats.
   - **Calculation settings**, such as **Calculation method**.
   - **Number formats**, such as **Amount format**. Each has an example, for example 1234.5678 shows as 1,234.568.

::: warning View only
You cannot change the values on the **Configuration** tab yourself. To change them, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help). Tell them the Cluster name, the Business Unit name and the value you want changed.
:::

## View and edit the hotel details

1. Open the **Hotel** tab.

   ![The Hotel tab](/screenshots/en/guide-bu-settings-02-hotel.png)

2. Look at **Contact** (**Hotel name**, **Phone**, **Email**) and **Location**. The **Open in Maps** link shows the address on a map.
3. To change a value, click it and type the new one.
4. Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom, or **Cancel** to throw your changes away.

What you change is **not saved at once**. If you leave the page without clicking **Save changes**, your changes are lost.

## View and edit the company details

The **Company** tab shows the company that is billed for this Business Unit. It has **Billing details** and **Billing address**. It works the same way as the **Hotel** tab: click a value to change it, then click **Save changes**. If the address is the same as the hotel's, click **Copy from hotel address**.

## Check that it worked

After you click **Save changes**, the bar at the bottom goes away and the new value shows on that tab.

## If something goes wrong

- You click a value on the **Configuration** tab and cannot change it. This is normal. The tab is view only. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- You change a value but no **Save changes** bar appears. Check that the value can really be edited. If it cannot, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
