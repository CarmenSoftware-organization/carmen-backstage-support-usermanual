# Invite a User to a Business Unit

This guide shows you how to invite a new person to the Cluster and choose which Business Units (a hotel or branch) they may use. Cluster admins can do this. Before you start, have the person's e-mail ready and know which Business Units they need. They do not need a Carmen Blue account yet. The link in the invitation lets them set a password and join in one step.

## Steps

1. Open **Users** in the left menu.

   ![The Users page](/screenshots/en/guide-invite-user-01-users-page.png)

2. Click **Invite user**, top right.

3. In **Email**, type the person's e-mail.

4. Choose the **Cluster role**: **Admin** (can look after the Cluster) or **User** (an ordinary member).

   ![The Invite user box](/screenshots/en/guide-invite-user-02-dialog.png)

5. Under **Business unit access**, tick the Business Units they may use.

6. Under each ticked Business Unit, choose their role there. If you want, tick **Default** on one of them.

   ![The invitation filled in](/screenshots/en/guide-invite-user-03-filled.png)

7. Click **Send**.

## Check that it worked

A message **Invitation sent** appears at the top. Open the **Invitations** tab and you will see the e-mail in the list. The status **Pending** means they have not answered yet.

![The Invitations tab](/screenshots/en/guide-invite-user-04-invitations.png)

## Resend or cancel an invitation

1. Open the **Invitations** tab.
2. Click **...** at the end of that invitation's row.
3. Click **Resend** to send it again, or **Revoke** to cancel it.
4. If you revoke, read the question and confirm. The person can no longer accept that invitation.

## If something goes wrong

- **Invitation already pending** means you already invited this e-mail and nobody has answered. Look in the **Invitations** tab and use **Resend** instead.
- **Invitation rate limit reached** means you sent too many in a short time. Wait a little and try again.
- You cannot find the Business Unit you need in the list. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
