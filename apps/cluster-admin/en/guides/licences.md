# Check Licenses and Expiry

This guide shows you how to read the **Licenses** page: what the Cluster has, whether anything is close to ending, and how seats are split by Business Unit (a hotel or branch). Cluster admins can do this. The page is for reading only.

## Steps

1. Open **Licenses** in the left menu.

   ![The Licenses page](/screenshots/en/guide-licences-01-overview.png)

2. Look at the two cards at the top.
   - **BUSINESS UNITS**, for example **4 / 5 licensed**, is how many Business Units you use out of those bought.
   - **SEATS**, for example **11 / 13 licensed** and **2 seats open**. To learn how to read it, see [Manage Seats](/en/guides/manage-seats).

3. Look at **SUBSCRIPTIONS** (the main license of each Business Unit). The top line says how many are in force, for example **3 of 3 in force**. The bar shows the period covered. The line under it gives the end date and the days left, such as **430 days left**. Below, each subscription is listed with its dates and short codes for its features.

4. Look at **INTERFACE LICENCES** (connections to other systems, such as a POS). Each row is one connection. **No expiry** means it has no end date.

5. Scroll down to **SEATS BY BUSINESS UNIT**. There is one row per Business Unit with its seats, its number of licenses and when they end. **Not purchased** means that Business Unit has no seats of its own.

6. Open the folded sections if you want more detail. **BU QUOTA LICENCES** shows how many Business Units the Cluster may have. **BUSINESS UNIT RANKING** shows the order in which Business Units are covered when the quota is not enough.

## Check whether something is close to ending

Look at **SUBSCRIPTIONS** and **INTERFACE LICENCES**. When licenses will end soon, a note such as **2 expiring soon** appears, and the days left are shown where they apply. Look at the last column of **SEATS BY BUSINESS UNIT** to see when each unit ends.

## See the licenses of one Business Unit

Click the Business Unit's name in the table, or open the Business Unit and go to its **Licenses** tab. It has the sub-tabs **Seats**, **Modules** and **Interface**.

![The Licenses tab of a Business Unit](/screenshots/en/guide-licences-02-bu-licences.png)

The **Seats** sub-tab shows the seats from this unit (**This business unit**) plus those from other units (**From other business units**). Together they make the Cluster pool, and it shows how many are **in use**.

::: warning
The **Manage licences** link at the top right of this tab does **not** let you add or change licenses. It only opens the **Licenses** page you are reading. Adding or renewing licenses is done by Carmen support. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) well before the end date.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- You see **End date required** on a license. That means no end date is recorded yet. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- A Business Unit shows **Not purchased** for seats. Its users take seats from the Cluster pool. If you need more seats, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
