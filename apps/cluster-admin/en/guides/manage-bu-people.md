# Manage People in a Business Unit

This guide shows you how to change a member's role in the Cluster and how to remove a member. Cluster admins can do this. Before you start, know the name or e-mail of the person.

## Change a member's role

1. Open **Users** in the left menu.
2. Type their name or e-mail in **Search members...** to find them.
3. Click **...** at the end of their row.

   ![The row menu](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-bu-people-01-row-menu.png)

4. Click **Make Admin** or **Make User** (the menu shows the role they do not have yet).

A message **Role updated** appears and the **Cluster Role** column changes.

![The role changed](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-bu-people-02-role-changed.png)

## Remove a member from the Cluster

::: warning
**Remove** takes the person out of the whole Cluster, not just one Business Unit. Check the name in the question before you confirm.
:::

::: tip If you need the seat back
Removing someone may not give their seat back to the Cluster straight away. If you are removing them to make room for someone new, first **Release seat** for them (see [Manage Seats](/en/guides/manage-seats)), then remove them. If the seat count still does not go down, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
:::

1. Click **...** at the end of their row.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Read the question, for example Remove "name" from this cluster? If it is the right person, click **Remove**. If not, click **Cancel**.

   ![Confirm the removal](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-bu-people-03-remove-confirm.png)

## Check that it worked

A message **Member removed** appears and the person is no longer in the member list. The number next to **Members** goes down by one.

![The member removed](/screenshots/en/guide-manage-bu-people-04-removed.png)

## See who is in one Business Unit

The **Users** page shows everyone in the Cluster. To see only one Business Unit, open that Business Unit and go to its **Users** tab. There you can change a person's role or status in that unit, or take them out of that unit. See [A Business Unit](/en/screens/business-unit-detail).

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot find the person. Clear the search, or click the **All members** filter. Someone who has not accepted an invitation yet is in the **Invitations** tab instead.
- If you cannot do it or are not sure, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
