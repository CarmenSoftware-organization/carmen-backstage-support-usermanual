# Sign In

You sign in to the Cluster Admin view on the same Carmen Blue sign-in page that everyone uses. There is no separate address or separate account.

## Sign in

1. Open the Carmen Blue sign-in page. Its heading is **Sign in**. The text under it mentions "Carmen Blue Backstage". This is the right page.
2. Type your e-mail address or username in **Email or username**.
3. Type your password in **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

If your company has turned on Google sign-in, you can click **Continue with Google** instead.

## What you see next

- If you look after **one Cluster** (a group of hotels run together), you go straight into that Cluster. You land on the [Cluster](/en/screens/cluster) screen.
- If you look after **more than one Cluster**, you first see the list of your Clusters. Pick one. See [Choose a Cluster](/en/getting-started/choose-cluster).

If your account has no Cluster Admin access, you will not see these screens. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) to ask for access.

## If you also have other access in Carmen Blue

Some people use other parts of Carmen Blue as well. If that is you, open the user menu (your name, top right) and choose **Cluster Admin view** to come to the screens in this manual.

## Signing out

Open the user menu (your name, top right) and choose **Log out**.
