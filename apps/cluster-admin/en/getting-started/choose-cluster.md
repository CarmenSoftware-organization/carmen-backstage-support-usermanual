# Choose a Cluster

A **Cluster** (a group of hotels managed together) is the top level in the Cluster Admin view. Everything you see belongs to one Cluster at a time.

## When you see this screen

You see the list of Clusters only if you look after **more than one** Cluster. If you look after just one, Carmen Blue skips this screen and opens that Cluster for you.

![The Cluster Admin screen with one card for each Cluster you look after](/screenshots/en/choose-cluster.png)

The page is called **Cluster Admin** and says **Choose a cluster to administer.** Each Cluster you look after is a card with its name.

## Open a Cluster

1. Click the card of the Cluster you want. You can also press Enter when the card is selected.
2. You land on the [Cluster](/en/screens/cluster) screen of that Cluster.

## Switch to another Cluster later

Once you are inside a Cluster, you do not need to come back to this list. In the top bar, next to the breadcrumb, there is a small box with the Cluster's short name and an up-and-down arrow. Click it, then choose or search for another Cluster. This box only appears if you look after more than one Cluster.

## If the list is empty

If the screen says **No clusters to administer**, your account is not set up to look after any Cluster. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).

If you are told that you no longer administer a Cluster, your access to it was removed. Choose another Cluster with **Back to my clusters**, or [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) to have it restored.
