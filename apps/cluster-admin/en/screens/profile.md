# Profile

**Where to find it:** click your name in the top bar > **Profile**.

This screen is about your own account. You see your details and the Business Units linked to your account. You can change your own details and your password. You cannot change the e-mail address.

![The Profile screen](/screenshots/en/profile-overview.png)

The page says **Manage your account settings and preferences**.

## What you can do here

- Check your name, telephone number and e-mail address.
- Change your details with **Edit**.
- Change your password with **Change Password**.
- See which Business Units are linked to your account.

## What is on the screen

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile Overview** | Your picture (your initials), your name and e-mail address, **Member since** and your **Account ID**. Give the Account ID to Carmen support if they ask for it. |
| **Profile Information** | **Alias Name**, **First Name**, **Middle Name**, **Last Name**, **Telephone** and **Email Address**. The e-mail address says **Email cannot be changed**. |
| **Business Units** | A card for each Business Unit assigned to your account, with its code and status. |

## Common tasks

### Change your details

1. Click **Edit**.
2. Change the fields you need.
3. Save your changes. To leave without saving, cancel.

### Change your password

1. Click **Change Password**.
2. Follow the steps on the screen.

## Cautions

- If your e-mail address is wrong, you cannot fix it here. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- The Business Units listed are only those linked to your account. To see or change who works in a Business Unit, open it from [Business Units](/en/screens/business-units).
