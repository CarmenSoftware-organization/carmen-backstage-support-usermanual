# Cluster

**Where to find it:** sidebar > **Cluster**. This is also the first screen you see after you open a Cluster.

This screen is the summary of your Cluster (a group of hotels managed together). It shows how many Business Units and seats you use, who looks after the Cluster, and the Cluster's name and logo.

![The Cluster screen](/screenshots/en/cluster-overview.png)

## What you can do here

- See how many Business Units (hotels) and seats the Cluster uses compared with what it is licensed for.
- Open any Business Unit from the list, or click **View all** to see all of them.
- See who the Cluster's administrators are, and click **View all** to open the [Users](/en/screens/users) screen.
- Change the Cluster's name, short name and logo with **Edit**.

## What is on the screen

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Title and the line under it | The Cluster's name. The line under it says **Tenant group**. This is a fixed label that every Cluster has. |
| **BUSINESS UNITS** | For example **4 / 5 licensed** and **1 of 5 quota free**. The Cluster uses 4 of the 5 Business Units it is licensed for. 1 more can be added. |
| **SEATS** | For example **11 / 13 licensed** and **2 seats open**. 11 seats are in use out of 13. Each bar is one seat. |
| **Business units** | The Business Units in the Cluster, with their short code. |
| **People** | The Cluster's **Administrators**, and a count of members without admin rights. |
| **Identity** | **Name**, **Alias name** (a short name of up to 3 characters) and **Status**. |
| **Branding** | The Cluster's logo. It is shown in the sidebar, in the Cluster switcher and in lists across Carmen Blue. If there is no logo, Carmen Blue shows the first letters of the name. |

## Common tasks

### Change the Cluster's name, short name or logo

1. Click **Edit**, top right.
2. Click the value you want to change in **Identity**, and type the new one.
3. To change the logo, use the upload button in **Branding** and choose a picture file.
4. Click **Save changes**. To leave without saving, click **Cancel**.

You cannot change **Status** yourself. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) if the status is wrong.

### See which Business Units are in the Cluster

Click **View all** next to **Business units**, or click a Business Unit's name to open it. See [Business Units](/en/screens/business-units).

## Cautions

::: warning
The name and logo you save here are shown to every person in the Cluster, in the sidebar and in lists across Carmen Blue.
:::

- You cannot add a Business Unit or add seats from this screen. Both are set by Carmen. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- If the Cluster uses more Business Units than it is licensed for, the extra ones can only be read, not changed. See [Licenses](/en/screens/licences).
