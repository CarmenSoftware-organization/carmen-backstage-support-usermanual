# Users

**Where to find it:** sidebar > **Users**.

This screen is about the people in your Cluster. It shows who is a member, who holds a **seat** (the right to use Carmen Blue), and which invitations are still waiting. The page says **Manage members and pending invitations for this cluster**.

![The Users screen](/screenshots/en/users-list.png)

## What you can do here

- See how many seats are used and how many are open.
- Invite a new person with **Invite user**.
- Find a member with **Search members...**.
- Change a member's role between **Admin** and **User**.
- Release a seat from a member, or restore it.
- Remove a member from the Cluster.
- Send an invitation again, or cancel it.

## The seat bar

At the top, **CLUSTER SEATS** shows for example **11 / 13 licensed** and **2 seats open**. Each bar is one seat. If more people hold seats than the Cluster is licensed for, a note tells you how many users are beyond the licensed seat count.

::: warning
If seats in use are more than the licensed seats, a warning asks you to release seats. While this is so, Carmen Blue cannot save changes in the Cluster's Business Units. Release the extra seats, or [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) to add seats.
:::

## The Members tab

The tab **Members** has a number: how many members the Cluster has.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **#** | The row number. |
| **Name** and **Email** | The person. |
| **Cluster Role** | **Admin** (can look after the Cluster) or **User** (an ordinary member). |
| **Seat** | The Business Units where the person holds a seat, or a note if they have none. |
| **Created** and **Updated** | When the membership was made and last changed. |

Above the table, three buttons filter the list: **All members**, **Holds seat** and **No seat**. Each shows a count.

The **...** button at the end of a row opens a menu:

- **Make Admin** or **Make User** changes the person's Cluster Role.
- **Release seat** or **Restore seat** (only when it applies).
- **Remove** takes the person out of the Cluster.

## The Invitations tab

The tab **Invitations** lists the invitations you have sent. The columns are **Email**, **Cluster Role**, **Status**, **Expires** and **Invited**. The status is **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Declined** or **Revoked**.

The **...** menu on a row has **Resend** (send the invitation again) and **Revoke** (cancel it).

## Common tasks

### Invite a person

1. Click **Invite user**, top right.
2. Type their address in **Email**. They do not need a Carmen Blue account yet. The invitation link lets them set a password and join.
3. Choose their **Cluster role**: **Admin** or **User**.
4. Under **Business unit access**, tick the Business Units they may use. For each one, choose a role. You may tick **Default** on one of them.
5. Click **Send**.

The person now appears in the **Invitations** tab as **Pending**.

### Release a seat

1. Find the person. Click **...** at the end of their row.
2. Click **Release seat**.
3. Read the message and confirm.

The person stops using a seat, and their access to the Cluster's Business Units is switched off until you click **Restore seat**. If it is your own account, you lose your own access, so be careful.

### Restore a seat

1. Click **...** on the person's row, then **Restore seat**, and confirm.
2. If no seat is free, you get a message that the seat limit is reached. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) to add seats.

### Change a person's role

Click **...** on their row, then **Make Admin** or **Make User**. A message confirms the change.

## Cautions

::: warning
**Remove** takes the person out of the whole Cluster. Check the name in the question before you confirm.
:::

- Seats cannot be added here. [Contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- An invitation that was **Revoked** can no longer be accepted.
- Some seat holders may not appear in the member list because their Cluster membership is not active. Free their seats from the **Users** tab of the Business Unit. See [A Business Unit](/en/screens/business-unit-detail).
