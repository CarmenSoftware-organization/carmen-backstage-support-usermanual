# Business Units

**Where to find it:** sidebar > **Business Units**.

A **Business Unit** (one hotel or branch in your Cluster) is the place where staff, seats and licenses are kept. This screen lists all the Business Units in the Cluster. Open one to see and change its details.

![The list of Business Units](/screenshots/en/business-units-list.png)

The page is called **Business Units** and says **Manage the business units in this cluster**.

## What you can do here

- Find a Business Unit with **Search business units...**.
- Show only active or only inactive Business Units with **Filters**.
- Sort the list by clicking a column title.
- Save the list as a file with **Export**.
- Open a Business Unit by clicking its name. See [A Business Unit](/en/screens/business-unit-detail).

## The columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **#** | The row number. |
| **Name** | The Business Unit's name. Click it to open the Business Unit. |
| **HQ** | A mark if this is the head office. |
| **Status** | **Active** or **Inactive**. |
| **Created** | How long ago it was created, and by whom. |
| **Updated** | How long ago it was last changed, and by whom. |

At the bottom you can pick how many rows to show (**10**, **25**, **50** or **100**) and move between pages.

## Common tasks

### Open a Business Unit

1. Type part of the name in **Search business units...** if the list is long.
2. Click the name of the Business Unit.

### Show only active Business Units

1. Click **Filters**.
2. Choose **Active**. Click **Clear** to show all again.

## Cautions

- There is no button to add a Business Unit. Carmen creates them. If your Cluster needs another one, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help).
- If the Cluster has more Business Units than it is licensed for, the screen tells you how many. Those Business Units are read-only until more quota is added. See [Licenses](/en/screens/licences).
