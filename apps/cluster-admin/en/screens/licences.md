# Licenses

**Where to find it:** sidebar > **Licenses**. The page title is the Cluster's name with **Licences** under it. Some titles use "licence" and the menu uses "license". They mean the same.

This screen shows every license your Cluster holds and when each one ends. It is for reading. You use it to check that nothing is about to run out.

![The Licenses screen](/screenshots/en/licences-overview.png)

## What you can do here

- See Business Unit and seat usage at the top, as on the [Cluster](/en/screens/cluster) screen.
- Check each Business Unit's subscription and when it ends.
- Check the interface licenses.
- See which Business Units have no seats yet.
- Click a row to open that Business Unit's own license tab.

## What is on the screen

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **BUSINESS UNITS** and **SEATS** | How many Business Units and seats are used out of what is licensed. |
| **SUBSCRIPTIONS** | For example **3 of 3 in force**. The bar shows the period covered, and the line under it shows the end date and the days left, such as **430 days left**. Under it, each Business Unit's subscriptions are listed with their dates and number of features. |
| **INTERFACE LICENCES** | For example **4 of 4 in force**. Each row is one interface (a connection to another system) for a Business Unit. **No expiry** means it has no end date. |
| **SEATS BY BUSINESS UNIT** | One row per Business Unit with its seats, its number of licenses and when they end. **Not purchased** means the Business Unit has no seats. The heading says how many Business Units have none. |
| **BU QUOTA LICENCES** | A closed section. Open it to see how many Business Units the Cluster may have, with start date, end date, status and reference. |
| **BUSINESS UNIT RANKING** | A closed section. Open it to see the order in which Business Units are covered when quota runs short: HQ first, then the oldest. A Business Unit marked **Beyond quota** is read-only. |

A card that says for example **2 expiring soon** is telling you that two licenses end soon.

## Common tasks

### Check what is about to end

1. Open **Licenses**.
2. Look at **SUBSCRIPTIONS** and **INTERFACE LICENCES** for **expiring soon** and the days left.
3. In **SEATS BY BUSINESS UNIT**, look at the last column for each Business Unit's end date.

### See the licenses of one Business Unit

Click its row. You land on the **Licenses** tab of that Business Unit. See [A Business Unit](/en/screens/business-unit-detail).

## Cautions

::: warning
You cannot add, renew or change a license on any screen of this manual. Carmen does that. When a license is about to end, or you need more seats, Business Units or interfaces, [contact Carmen support](/en/getting-help) well before the end date.
:::
