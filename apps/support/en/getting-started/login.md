# Sign In

This page explains how to sign in to Carmen Blue Backstage (the admin app for platform administrators), how to sign out, and what the messages on the sign-in screen mean.

## What you need

- The web address of Carmen Blue Backstage (ask the team that runs your system).
- An account: your **Email or username** and your **Password**.
- Your account must have a **Backstage Role** (a set of permissions that says what you can do in this app) or be a **Super Admin** (someone who can do everything). Without one, the app will not let you in. See [Messages you may see](#messages).

## The sign-in screen

![The sign-in screen](/screenshots/en/login.png)

- Left side: the product name and the system status. **All systems operational** means the system is ready to use.
- Right side: the sign-in form.
- Top-right corner: the language button, to switch between Thai and English.
- Bottom: the version number. Give it to support when you report a problem.

## Sign in with email or username

1. Open the Carmen Blue Backstage web address.
2. To change the language, click the language button in the top-right corner.
3. Fill in **Email or username**.
4. Fill in **Password**.
5. Click **Sign in**.

When it works, you land on the **Dashboard**. See [The Main Screen](./main-screen).

If you had opened a link to another page first, or you have to sign in again because your session timed out, you are taken back to that page after you sign in. You do not need to find it again.

If you leave a box empty, a message appears under it, such as **Username is required** or **Password is required**.

## Sign in with Google

The **Continue with Google** button is always shown, but you can only click it when an administrator has turned Google sign-in on. If the button looks faded, it is off. Use your email and password instead.

1. Click **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose your Google account on the Google page.
3. You come back to Carmen Blue Backstage, signed in.

Your Google email must match the email of an existing Carmen Blue Backstage account. The app does not create new accounts by itself.

## Messages you may see {#messages}

| Message on screen | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invalid email/username or password.** | The email, username or password is wrong. | Check upper and lower case and your keyboard language, then try again. |
| **Access denied** followed by **Access Denied. You are not authorized to access this platform.** | Your password is right, but your account has no role in this app yet. | Ask a Super Admin to give you a role on the [Backstage Users](/en/screens/backstage/user-backstage) screen. |
| **Too many login attempts. Please try again later.** and the button changes to **Please wait** | Too many failed tries in a row, so the app paused sign-in for a while. | Wait a moment, then change any box. The button works again. |
| **Unable to login. Please try again later.** | The system has a temporary problem. | Wait and try again. If it still fails, tell support. |
| **No Carmen Blue Backstage account exists for this Google email address.** | The Google email you chose has no account here. | Ask an administrator to add you first, or choose another Google account. |
| **This email address conflicts with another account.** | The email clashes with another account. | Contact your administrator. |
| **Too many sign-in attempts. Please wait a moment and try again.** | Too many Google sign-in tries in a row, so the app paused it for a while. | Wait a moment, then click **Continue with Google** again. |
| **Google sign-in is turned off.** | An administrator has not turned Google sign-in on. | Sign in with your email and password. |
| **Google sign-in failed. Please try again.** | Something went wrong on the way to or from Google. | Try again. |

## Sign out

1. Click your name in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Click **Log out**.

If you share the computer with others, always sign out when you finish.

## Forgot your password

The sign-in screen has no "reset password" button. Ask an administrator to set a new password for you. Once you are signed in, you can change your own password on the [Profile](/en/screens/profile) screen.
